The form

Start from

Each block is a section: it plays its floor a number of times, and grows wider with every bar. Drag blocks to reorder them, drag one off the lane to remove it, drag its right edge to repeat its floor more or fewer times. Hold Alt while dragging to drop a copy. Its chips show the treatment, Toby's five moves: a new instrument, a new octave, a doubling on top, adding or stripping bass and drums. Tap a chip to take that move off.

Floors

A floor is the bass walk under the bar, one note per bar. Megalovania uses four, built from only five notes: B♭, B, C, C♯ and D. The last two are the short walks where the riff comes back in before the loop restarts. Drag a floor by its name onto a block to stand that section on it. Drag a note up or down to change it.

The bar

Sixteen sixteenth-notes. The first two notes are the head: they always take the pitch of the floor under the bar. The rest is the tail. Tap an empty column to add a note, drag a note up or down to change its pitch, tap a note to remove it.

What the transcription shows

The riff plays in 32 of the loop's 78 bars in Jay Reichard's transcription (36 in fakt13's and Zumi's, which also put it under bars 21–24). The other bars carry three different tunes, but all of them stand on the same four floors, so the bar's harmony never leaves.

At bar 49 the riff comes back over a floor it has never stood on, B♭ C B B · B♭ C D D. In its first bar the head stays on D while the bass plays B♭. At bar 71 it returns again and strips back to the riff alone just before the loop restarts.
