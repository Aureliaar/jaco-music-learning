The bar

Sixteen sixteenth-notes. The first two notes are the head: they always take the pitch of the floor under the bar. The rest is the tail. Click a column to move its note to that pitch, click the note again to remove it.

Floors

A floor is the bass walk under the bar, one note per bar. Megalovania uses four, built from only five notes: B♭, B, C, C♯ and D. The last two rows are the short walks where the riff comes back in before the loop restarts.

The form

Each section plays its floor a number of times. Its treatment is Toby's five moves: a new instrument (voice), a new octave, a doubling on top, and adding or stripping bass and drums.

What the transcription shows

The riff plays in 32 of the loop's 78 bars in Jay Reichard's transcription (36 in fakt13's and Zumi's, which also put it under bars 21–24). The other bars carry three different tunes, but all of them stand on the same four floors, so the bar's harmony never leaves.

At bar 49 the riff comes back over a floor it has never stood on, B♭ C B B · B♭ C D D. In its first bar the head stays on D while the bass plays B♭. At bar 71 it returns again and strips back to the riff alone just before the loop restarts.
